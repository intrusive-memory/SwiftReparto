# Cast

## Major Characters

### NARRATOR

Deep authoritative British baritone. Warm, commanding documentary narrator.
Rich resonance, measured pacing.

### ESPECTRO FAMILIAR

Methodical, unhurried male voice with ancient wisdom. Calm and deliberate
cadence, as though this entity has existed for thousands of years and will
exist for thousands more. Never rushed. Precise articulation. Quiet authority
without force.

### THE PRACTITIONER

Younger adult male voice, still finding his footing somewhere between fear
and curiosity. The bridge generation — analog childhood, digital adulthood.
Brings the wound; asks the questions.
